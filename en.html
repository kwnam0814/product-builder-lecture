💡 Tips for Healthy Eating

Choosing what to eat is important, but how you eat matters too. Try these tips:

  • Balanced Diet: Aim for a mix of carbohydrates, proteins, and fats. Always try to include vegetables.
  • Watch Sodium: When eating stews or soups, try to eat mostly the solid ingredients to reduce sodium intake.
  • Eat Slowly: Taking at least 20 minutes to eat helps you feel full and prevents overeating.
  • Hydration: Drinking water before or after meals aids digestion (but avoid too much during meals).
